Count every strike thrown in this short observation window of an MMA fight, round or sparring footage. Inspect the supplied video frame by frame and use the attached fighter crops to tell the fighters apart. Strikes are punches (strike_punch), kicks (strike_kick), knees (strike_knee), elbows (strike_elbow) and any strike delivered while either fighter is on the ground (ground_strike). Include strikes inside the clinch and against the cage, short knees and body shots included. Each strike in a combination is its own entry: a jab-cross-hook is three entries. Feints, pawing that never extends, pushes, frames, grips and stance changes are not strikes.

Only live action counts. If the window shows a broadcast replay, slow motion, a round break, walkouts, the referee holding the fighters apart, or the decision, return no strikes for that footage.

For each strike give at_ms: the absolute source-video integer millisecond of contact, or of full extension when it misses. The window's start_ms and end_ms are absolute source-video times; at_ms must lie inside the window. Give the fighter who throws it (actor_player_id from the crops, or actor_sentinel unclear/contested when you cannot tell, never both), the target (head, body or leg: where it landed or was aimed), the outcome (successful = visibly landed; unsuccessful = missed, blocked or parried; unclear = contact not visible), significant (true for every strike thrown at striking range and for power strikes in the clinch or on the ground; false for light pawing or pitty-pat strikes), knockdown (true only when this strike itself drops the opponent or takes their footing), and short visual evidence. Order strikes by at_ms. Echo the supplied window_index. Return an empty strikes array only when no strike is thrown. Return only the required JSON schema.
