## Workaround: `max_missing_frames=999999` to bypass mid-clip suspend

**Smoke attempt #1 (saved as `smoke_attempt1_suspended/`) revealed a second
human-in-the-loop gate**: when SAM2 reports a track lost for >15 frames
(`tracking_max_missing_frames=15` default), the worker uploads a `track_mid_loss`
checkpoint and flips the job to `AWAITING_CORRECTION`, sitting up to 24 h for a human to
submit corrected boxes via `POST /jobs/{id}/resume`. On this fixture, track 2 was lost at
frame 759 (~12.8 s of content), so the job suspended at 31.7% progress.

Per the M0 task ("config and env vars only — no source edits"), the request schema
already exposes `max_missing_frames` (`service/models.py:58`). Setting it to a sentinel
large value disables the mid-track suspend. This makes the M0 measurement **worst-case
unattended**: any re-id confusion is suppressed, so the resulting `analysis_final.json`
may have noisy tracks across re-id boundaries. As an M0 oracle this is acceptable — it
captures the pure compute cost of the unmodified pipeline. Production deploys will keep
a sane `max_missing_frames` and will still hit the suspend for tracking re-IDs (a
separate concern from the speedup plan).

## Peak resource usage

`nvidia-smi-samples.csv` is polled every 30 s. GB10 has **unified memory and no per-process VRAM reporting** (nvidia-smi returns `N/A` for `memory.used` / `memory.total`); the closest proxy is system RSS (`free -m`) which is included in the sample CSV. GPU `utilization.gpu` is sampled and is the load-share signal.

## M0 branch decision per plan

**Verdict: full M1 + M2 (no shortcut).**

Evidence:

1. **Smoke total ≈ 1.7 h for a 30 s clip.** Linearly extrapolated full-clip total ≈ **7.4 h**. The plan's threshold for "skip M1-M2" is ≤ 30 min end-to-end, and for "selective M1-M2" is 30 min - 2 h. Both are blown by an order of magnitude.
2. **No single stage > 60 % of wall, but two stages dominate together (~98 %).** UPSCALE+ANALYZE alone is ~82 % of the smoke wall time; TRACK is ~16 %. Both need attention — there is no single bottleneck whose targeted fix shrinks total wall under budget.
3. **All the levers in M1 and M2 are required, not optional:**
   - **S1 (CUDA hardening + batched ESRGAN)**: ESRGAN measured at ~3 frames/5s ≈ 0.6 fps. With batched x4 forward passes + channels_last + tf32 + cudnn.benchmark, the plan's expected 10-30× plus 2-3× from batching is exactly what's needed to drag upscale stage below 20 min.
   - **S3 (decouple upscale loop from analyzer)**: analyzer's per-window Gemini call (~30s) currently blocks the upscale loop after every 30-frame window. Decoupling via an asyncio queue is necessary to reach the projected upscale floor.
   - **S4 (analyzer async + pipelining + opt-in fanout)**: 119 windows × sync Gemini call is the second-largest cost. Pipelining + fanout at G=2-4 is needed if accuracy holds.
   - **S5 (smarter upscale target)**: target_size 1024 → 768 plus a pre-downsample is a ~3-4× FLOPs cut on upscale — free, no accuracy risk.
   - **S6 (sequential VideoCapture)**: per-frame `cv2.VideoCapture.set(POS_FRAMES)` random seek on VP9 (fixture is VP9!) is exactly the kind of long-GOP codec where this hurts most.
   - **S7 (SAM2 step_size=120 + offload off)** and **S8 (RTMPose on CUDA)**: required to bring tracking under 7 min for the full clip (smoke pace projects 75 min).
4. **No pathological-stage finding *yet***, but per-frame `empty_cache()` and per-frame JPEG writes are still in the loop — both are pure waste at the throughput required. They'll show up in the M1 profile pass.
5. **Cross-job concurrency (S10)** is independent of per-job wall time and can be deferred — but the single-job-concurrency invariant in `2026-03-15-single-job-concurrency` is correctly flagged for supersession in M3 since GB10's 128 GB unified memory has plenty of headroom for ≥2 concurrent jobs.

The smoke-run `analysis_final.json` (once the smoke job lands its UPLOAD checkpoint) will serve as the **standard-mode oracle for plan Section D's regression harness**, with the caveat documented above about `max_missing_frames=999999`.
